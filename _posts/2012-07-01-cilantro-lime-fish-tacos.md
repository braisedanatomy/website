A taco in hand is worth two...on the grill? Actually, that's not true at all. Two tacos on the grill, means you will shortly have two tacos in your hand. Yummmm.

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/tacos2.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-3991" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/tacos2-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a><em>(Adapted from</em><span style="color: #ff0000;"><em> <a href="http://www.ourbestbites.com/2010/05/cilantro-lime-vinaigrette-grilled-fish-tacos/"><span style="color: #ff0000;">Our Best Bites</span></a></em></span><em>)</em>

Ingredients:
<ul>
	<li>1/2 cup fresh lime juice (about 3-4 limes)</li>
	<li>1/2 cup rice vinegar</li>
	<li>4-5 garlic cloves</li>
	<li>1 tsp kosher salt</li>
	<li>2 tsp sugar</li>
	<li>1/2 cup canola oil</li>
	<li>1 cup cilantro</li>
	<li>Tilapia fillets</li>
	<li>Corn tortillas</li>
	<li><a href="/2012/06/03/cabbage-and-apple-coleslaw/">Cabbage and Apple Coleslaw</a></li>
</ul>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Combine the lime juice, vinegar, garlic, salt, and sugar in a food processor. </span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">With the processor running, slowly add the oil to form an emulsion.  </span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Add the cilantro to the marinade and pulse so the cilantro becomes lightly minced.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Pour the marinade over the fish and let sit for an hour (or longer).</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Over a medium-hot grill, grill the fish for 4-5 minutes per side.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Serve on a corn tortilla with <a href="/2012/06/03/cabbage-and-apple-coleslaw/">Cabbage and Apple Coleslaw</a> (or whatever other toppings you would like) and enjoy!</span></li>
</ol>
</div>
