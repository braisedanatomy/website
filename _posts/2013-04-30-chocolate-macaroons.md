Ingredients (makes about 30 cookies)
<ul>
	<li>2 large egg whites</li>
	<li>1/4 tsp salt</li>
	<li>1/3 cup sugar</li>
	<li>1 tsp vanilla</li>
	<li>1 1/2 cup sweetened coconut flakes</li>
	<li>1 1/2 cup chocolate chips</li>
</ul>
<ol>
	<li><span style="line-height: 15.994318008422852px;">Preheat oven to 325 degrees F.</span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 15.994318008422852px;">In a double boiler (or a <a href="http://bakingbites.com/2009/09/how-to-make-a-double-boiler/">makeshift double boiler</a>), melt <span style="text-decoration: underline;">1 cup</span> (save the other 1/2 cup for later) of your chocolate chips. Be sure to stir regularly so the chocolate doesn't burn. Cool chocolate to room temperature</span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 15.994318008422852px;">While the chocolate is cooling, beat the egg whites and salt in a CLEAN bowl (otherwise the eggs won't foam properly) with an electric mixer. Mix until the egg whites form soft peaks.  </span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 15.994318008422852px;">Slowly add the sugar and vanilla to the eggs and continue mixing until smooth.</span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 15.994318008422852px;">Fold in the melted chocolate and coconut.</span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 15.994318008422852px;">Fold in the remaining chocolate chips.</span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 15.994318008422852px;">Spoon the batter onto 2 cookie sheets lined with either parchment paper or a silicone mat. Space the cookies 1 1/2 inches apart.</span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 15.994318008422852px;">Bake for 10 minutes.</span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 15.994318008422852px;">Reverse the cookie sheets and bake for another 10 minutes.</span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 15.994318008422852px;">Cool cookies on a rack and enjoy!</span></li>
</ol>
