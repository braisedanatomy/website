<span style="font-size: 16px;">Ingredients (including hardware)</span>
<ul>
	<li>1/2 - 1 cup milk (I used almond milk, but I bet you can make even better foam with 2% cow's milk)</li>
	<li>A glass jar for microwaving that is at least double the volume of your milk</li>
	<li>A milk frother ($2.99 at IKEA in-store or <a href="http://www.worldmarket.com/product/multicolored-milk-frother-set-of-3.do">World Market</a>)</li>
	<li>Coffee (stronger than usual, because the un-frothed milk will dilute it out)</li>
</ul>
<h5>Pour the milk into the large glass jar, and froth with the milk frother.  Not much to it, just make sure you get a good head of foam; I can get the volume to increase by almost 100%.  Put the jar in the microwave and microwave on high for up to 1.5 minutes; watch the jar carefully and turn off the microwave if the foam head threatens to overflow the jar.  Take the jar out and pour the milk over your coffee; use a spoon to hold back the foam first, then use the spoon to scoop out the foam over the coffee.  Magic.</h5>
