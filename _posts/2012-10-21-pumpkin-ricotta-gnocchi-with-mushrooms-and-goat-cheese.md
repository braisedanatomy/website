As promised, here is the first recipe featuring homemade pumpkin purée. This is inspired by one of my favorite Cleveland restaurants - <a href="http://www.s-melissa.com/">Sweet Melissa</a>. While I don't for a second claim that this recipe is quite as good as their Pumpkin Sage Gnocchi, it's nevertheless pretty darn tasty.
<h5><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/series.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4216" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/series-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a></h5>
<h5><em><span style="color: #ff0000;">(Adapted from <a href="http://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/pumpkin_ricotta_gnocchi/"><span style="color: #ff0000;">Simply Recipes</span></a>)</span></em></h5>
Ingredients (makes 8 servings)
<ul>
	<li>2 cups pumpkin purée (<a href="/2012/10/16/sweet-and-sriracha-pumpkin-seeds-and-anatomy-of-a-pumpkin/">homemade</a> or from a can)</li>
	<li>1 1/2 cup ricotta</li>
	<li>3 eggs</li>
	<li>3 tsp kosher salt</li>
	<li>1/2 cup grated parmesan</li>
	<li>5-6 cups all-purpose flour</li>
	<li>2/3 cup butter + 1 tbs for mushrooms</li>
	<li>2 tsp dried sage</li>
	<li>5-6 cloves garlic, minced</li>
	<li>16 oz sliced mushrooms</li>
	<li>Goat Cheese (as much as you like)</li>
	<li>Salt and Pepper to taste</li>
</ul>
<ol>
	<li>In a large bowl, mix together the pumpkin, ricotta, eggs, salt, and parmesan.</li>
	<li>Add 2 cups of flour and mix well. Continue adding flour, 1 cup at a time, until the dough is manageable but still pretty sticky (it took me 6 cups of flour).</li>
	<li>Coat the dough with flour, knead into a large ball and cut it into 7-8 even pieces.</li>
	<li>Roll each section out into a long rope about 1 inch in diameter, and cut into 1/2 inch pieces.</li>
	<li>You can either freeze the uncooked gnocchi on a cookie sheet (make sure they’re not touching) or cook them right away by adding them to boiling water and letting them cook until they all float to the top (cook 1/3-1/2 of the total gnocchi at a time).</li>
	<li>While the gnocchi are cooking, sauté the mushrooms in 1 tbs butter...when it comes to adding the mushrooms, butter and garlic to the gnocchi, I recommend cooking everything in 2 batches (as described below).</li>
	<li>In a cast iron skillet, slowly brown 1/3 cup butter.</li>
	<li>Add 1 tsp sage and 1/2 of the garlic, and sauté until fragrant (be sure not to burn the garlic!).</li>
	<li>Add the salt, pepper, 1/2 of the cooked gnocchi and 1/2 of the mushrooms to the butter and garlic and continue cooking for 3-5 minutes.</li>
	<li>Serve the gnocchi and mushrooms topped with crumbled goat cheese.</li>
</ol>
