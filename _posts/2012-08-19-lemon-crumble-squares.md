I feel like this recipe doesn't even warrant an introduction. It's just that good. So let's just get on with the show...

(Adapted from <a href="http://thepioneerwoman.com/cooking/2010/03/creamy-lemon-crumb-squares/">The Pioneer Woman</a> and <a href="http://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/ina-garten/lemon-bars-recipe/index.html">Ina Garten</a>)

Ingredients:
<ul>
	<li>1 1/2 stick butter, softened</li>
	<li>3/4 brown sugar</li>
	<li>1 1/3 flour</li>
	<li>1 cup oats</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp salt</li>
	<li>1 tsp baking powder</li>
	<li>1.5 cups sugar</li>
	<li>1.5 cups lemon juice</li>
	<li>6 room temp eggs</li>
	<li>1 cup flour</li>
	<li>2 tbs zest</li>
</ul>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">In a large bowl, combine the butter and brown sugar with an electric mixer.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">In a separate bowl, sift together the flour, oats, salt and baking powder.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Combine the flour mixture with the butter and brown sugar.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Press half of the oat mixture into the bottom on an 9x13 in baking dish.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Let the crust bake for 10-15 minutes then set aside to cool.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">For the filling, whisk together the sugar, lemon juice, eggs, flour and zest.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Pour the filling over the bottom crust, top with the remaining crumble mixture, and bake for 30 minutes (until the filling is set). </span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Let cool then enjoy!</span></li>
</ol>
</div>
