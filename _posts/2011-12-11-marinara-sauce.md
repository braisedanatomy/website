<h5>This sauce is the first thing I ever really learned how to cook. Sure, I had made things before, but always from recipes. This sauce was different. I learned how to make it without a recipe, and it's reached the point where I can make from muscle memory. I let my brain take me as far as getting the ingredients out of the fridge, but then I shift into autopilot. Before I know it, I'm sitting down to a very big serving of pasta.</h5>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/pasta.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-3490" title="pasta" src="/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/pasta-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a>

What you need:
<ul>
	<li>2 28oz cans of crushed tomatoes (my dad and I love the Whole Foods brand)</li>
	<li>1-2 tbs olive oil</li>
	<li>4-5 cloves of garlic, minced</li>
	<li>1 tbs sugar</li>
	<li>1 tsp salt (add more as needed)</li>
	<li>1/4-1/3 cup minced <span style="text-decoration: underline;">fresh</span> basil</li>
	<li>1 lb capellini (angel hair) pasta</li>
	<li>Freshly grated parmesan cheese (<em>not optional</em>)</li>
</ul>
<div><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 24px;">What you need to do:</span></span></div>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">In a large sauce pan, sauté the garlic in olive oil until fragrant. </span></span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Add the crushed tomatoes to the sauce pan along with the sugar, salt and basil.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Bring to a boil on medium-high heat, then simmer on low for 20-30 minutes (b</span><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">e sure to taste the the sauce and add more salt/sugar as needed).</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">While the sauce simmers, cook your pasta and grate your parmesan cheese.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Combine the pasta with the sauce, top with cheese and enjoy!</span></li>
</ol>
