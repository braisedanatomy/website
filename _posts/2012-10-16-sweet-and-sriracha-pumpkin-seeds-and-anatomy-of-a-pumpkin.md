<h2><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Steps to breaking down a pumpkin</span></h2>
<ol>
	<li>Drive to the <a href="http://www.pattersonfarm.com/">pumpkin store</a>.</li>
	<li>Make a bee-line for the donuts.</li>
	<li>Gorge yourself on pumpkin and apple cider donuts and momentarily forget that you came here for pumpkins. <a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/donuts1.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4176" title="donuts1" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/donuts1-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a></li>
	<li>Remember the underlying reason for your visit when the pumpkins catch your eye.</li>
	<li>Wander over to the pumpkins at a slightly slower pace and try not to jostle the lead ball of donut sitting in the pit of your stomach. <a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/pumpkinpatch1.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4177" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/pumpkinpatch1-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a></li>
	<li>Pick your perfect pumpkin. <a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/pickpumpkin1.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4178" title="I'd like you to meet my sister Zoe. She thinks I'm a pumpkin." src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/pickpumpkin1-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a></li>
	<li>Drive home and try to ignore the slight resting tremor as the sugar surge sets in.</li>
	<li>Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F.</li>
	<li>Cut your pumpkin in half, scoop out the innards and set them aside for later.</li>
	<li>Place the two halves, cut side down, on cookie sheets and bake for 1.5 - 2 hours (or until the pumpkin is soft). <a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/pumpkin1.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4179" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/pumpkin1-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a></li>
	<li>While the pumpkin is cooking, separate the pumpkin seeds from the "connective tissue". <a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/innards.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4180" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/innards-1024x219.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="136" /></a></li>
	<li>Rinse the pumpkin seeds and spread out on a cookie sheet in a thin layer. <a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/dryingseeds1.jpg">
<img title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/dryingseeds1-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a></li>
	<li>Set aside the pumpkin seeds to dry overnight...only 12-14 short hours until you get to dig into some tasty Sweet and Sriracha Pumpkin Seeds. <a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/dryingseeds1.jpg">
</a></li>
	<li>Sit back and relax (while watching <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downton_Abbey">Downton Abbey</a> perhaps?) as your house fills with the mouth watering smell of baked pumpkin. <a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/bakedpumpkin1.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4182" title="bakedpumpkin1" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/bakedpumpkin1-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a></li>
	<li>Once the pumpkins are done baking, use a large spoon to scoop out the pumpkin meat.</li>
	<li>Either store the pumpkin as is or pureé 2 cups at a time in a blender.</li>
	<li>Refrigerate for use within the next couple days or freeze for later use...Recipes for your homemade pumpkin pureé to follow.</li>
</ol>

<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Sweet and Sriracha Pumpkin Seeds</span>

Ingredients:
<ul>
	<li>2 cups dried, raw pumpkin seeds</li>
	<li>1 tbs sriracha (more if you want spicier seeds)</li>
	<li>1 tbs brown sugar</li>
	<li>1 tbs soy sauce</li>
	<li>1 tbs olive oil</li>
</ul>
<ol>
	<li>Preheat the oven to 300 degrees F.</li>
	<li>Mix together the sriracha, brown sugar, soy sauce and olive oil.</li>
	<li>In a large bowl, toss together the pumpkin seeds with the sriracha mixture.</li>
	<li>Spread out the coated pumpkin seeds on a greased cookie sheet in a thin layer.</li>
	<li>Bake for 35-45 minutes, or till golden brown.</li>
</ol>
