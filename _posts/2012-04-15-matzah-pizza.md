<h5>Now I realize I'm exactly one week late with this recipe, but I'm going to make the argument that I'm right one time...For those of you who are familiar with this dry, tasteless piece of food masquerading as a holiday treat (aka matzah), you are also familiar with the all-to-common issue of being left with a large supply of this stuff once the holiday is over. Every year you promise yourself that you'll eat all of it during the holiday, yet every year you finish off the week only to find that mischievous final box (that you swear you already ate) hiding behind the toaster or in the cabinet or under the sink, and you think to yourself, "great...as much as I loathe you, I know I'm going to eat you." While I definitely recommend certain recipes like <a href="/2011/04/24/matzah-brei-fried-matzah/">Matzah Brei</a> as one option to help you get rid of those final sheets of paper-flavored cracker, this recipe is also a tasty option and you'll hardly realize recognize the starting ingredient by the time you sit down for dinner.</h5>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/sauce.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-3838" title="this is what I mean by covering the sauce with oregano." src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/sauce-1024x338.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="211" /></a><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/pizzas.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-3839" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/pizzas-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a>What you need:
<ul>
	<li>28 oz can of crushed tomatoes</li>
	<li>4-5 cloves of garlic, minced</li>
	<li>dried oregano</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp salt</li>
	<li>2 tsp sugar</li>
	<li>grated mozzarella</li>
	<li>grated parmesan</li>
	<li>toppings (I used banana peppers, fresh basil and sun dried tomatoes)</li>
</ul>
<div><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 24px;">What you need to do:</span></span></div>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Preheat your oven to 375 degrees.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">In a sauce pan, sauté the garlic in olive oil until it becomes fragrant (no more than 1 minute).</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Add your crushed tomatoes, salt and sugar, then sprinkle dried oregano on top of the sauce until it's covered with a fine layer (see image about). Stir until combined. (I know that sounds really unspecific, but trust me with this one. This is the best pizza sauce you've ever tried.)</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Heat on medium until it begins to bubble then turn down to low heat. Once it begins to simmer again, turn off the heat.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Place your matzah on a cooking sheet, cover with tomato sauce, cheese and the toppings of your choice.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Bake for 15 minutes, until the cheese has melted. Cut into wedges and enjoy!</span></li>
</ol>
</div>
