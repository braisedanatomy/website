<h5><span style="color: #000000; font-size: 1em; line-height: 1.5em;">I'm sure most Clevelanders and some Cleveland-ophiles have heard about the </span><a style="font-size: 1em;" href="http://fox8.com/2013/02/05/tour-inside-west-side-market-after-fire/">fire that took place early in the morning on January 30th inside the West Side Market</a><span style="color: #000000; font-size: 1em; line-height: 1.5em;">, a Cleveland food landmark built out of an old train station.  When visitors ask me about Cleveland, the WSM is the first thing I talk about.  Not only did I work there--I forged friendships there.</span></h5>
<img src="http://media.cleveland.com/plain_dealer_metro/photo/west-side-market-fire-interiorjpg-085815372832f062.jpg" alt="" width="614" height="406" />
