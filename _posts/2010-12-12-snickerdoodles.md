What you need:
<ul>
	<li>3 3/4 cups all purpose flour</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp baking soda</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp salt</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp cream of tartar</li>
	<li>1 cup (2 sticks) butter, softened</li>
	<li>2 cups sugar</li>
	<li>2 eggs</li>
	<li>1/4 cup milk</li>
	<li>1 tsp vanilla</li>
	<li>Cinnamon Sugar (4 tbs sugar and 1 tbs cinnamon)</li>
</ul>
What you need to do:
<ol>
	<li>Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F, and grease or line your cookie sheet.</li>
	<li>Stir together the flour, baking soda, cream of tartar and salt (please notice I did not mention sugar).</li>
	<li>Beat butter for 30 seconds, then add sugar and beat until fluffy (2-3 minutes).</li>
	<li>Add the eggs, milk and vanilla; beat well.</li>
	<li>Add the dry ingredients to butter mixture, and mix for another minute or so.</li>
	<li>Form dough into walnut-sized balls and roll in cinnamon sugar mixture.</li>
	<li>Place 2 inches apart on sheet and flatten with the bottom of drinking glass.</li>
	<li>Bake for about 8 minutes.</li>
	<li>Enjoy!</li>
</ol>
