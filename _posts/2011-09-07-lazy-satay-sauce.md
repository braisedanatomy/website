Ingredients
<ul>
	<li>crunchy peanut butter - start with 1/2 cup</li>
	<li>lime juice - 1 Tbsp at a time</li>
	<li>soy sauce - 1 tsp at a time</li>
	<li>chili powder - 1/4 tsp at a time</li>
</ul>
<h5>Start with the peanut butter, and add the other ingredients one part at a time.  You should be able to pick out all the flavors.  Add water if necessary to achieve that saucy consistency.</h5>
