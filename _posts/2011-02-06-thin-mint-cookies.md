What you need:
<ul>
	<li>12 oz (3 sticks) of unsalted butter (room temperature)</li>
	<li>1.5 cups powdered sugar</li>
	<li>1.5 tsp vanilla extract</li>
	<li>1.5 cups unsweetened cocoa powder</li>
	<li>1 tsp salt</li>
	<li>2 1/4 cups unbleached flour</li>
	<li>36 oz (3 bags) semisweet chocolate chips</li>
	<li>2-3 tsp mint extract (depending on how minting you want the cookies)</li>
</ul>
What you need to do:
<ol>
	<li>Preheat the oven to 350 degrees.</li>
	<li>Using a hand mixer (or standing mixer) cream the butter.</li>
	<li>Add the powdered sugar and continue to mix.</li>
	<li>Add the vanilla, salt and cocoa powder. Mix until it is the consistency of frosting.</li>
	<li>Add the flour and mix until there is no loose flour, but the dough is still crumbly. Be careful not to over mix the dough.</li>
	<li>Kneed the dough a few times and shape it into a ball.</li>
	<li>Place the ball in a plastic bag and flatten it to about 3/4 in thick.</li>
	<li>Chill the dough in the freezer for 20 min.</li>
	<li>Remove the dough from the freezer and cut it into 4 sections.</li>
	<li>Roll out each section individually by placing it between two sheets of plastic wrap and rolling it out to 1/8 inch with a rolling pin.</li>
	<li>Stamp out cookies with a 2 1/2 inch cookie cutter.</li>
	<li>Place cookies on a parchment lined cookie sheet and bake for 10 minutes. Allow cookies to cool before beginning the chocolate dipping process.</li>
	<li>To prepare the mint chocolate topping, melt the chocolate chips in a double broiler (I just put a pyrex bowl over a pot of boiling water), and once the chocolate is completely melted, add the peppermint 1/2 tsp at a time (be sure to taste after each addition).</li>
	<li>Drop each cookie in the bowl of melted chocolate and pass it back and forth between two forks to allow the excess chocolate to drip off. Once evenly coated, place the cookies on parchment paper to cool.</li>
	<li>Place the cookies in the refrigerator to allow the chocolate to harden.</li>
	<li>Enjoy!</li>
</ol>
