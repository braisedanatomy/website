You might have noticed a theme with the last couple posts...that's because avocados were on sale. Maybe I'll get more creative with the next one, but in the meantime, enjoy this tasty (and heavily discounted) twist on <a href="/2010/08/29/tomato-onion-and-basil-bruschetta/">bruschetta</a>.

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/unmixedmixed.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-3925" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/unmixedmixed-754x1024.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="869" /></a>

What you need:
<ul>
	<li>2 avocados, diced</li>
	<li>2 tomatoes, diced</li>
	<li>1 large clove of garlic, minced</li>
	<li>1/4 cup basil, minced</li>
	<li>1/4 cup mint, minced</li>
	<li>1 tbs lime juice</li>
	<li>salt and pepper to taste</li>
</ul>
<div><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 24px;">What you need to do:</span></span></div>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Combine all of the ingredients listed above.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Serve on toasted bread (here's another great place to use <a href="/2012/05/13/smoked-salmon-sandwich/">JJ's day old bread</a>!), and enjoy!</span></li>
</ol>
</div>
