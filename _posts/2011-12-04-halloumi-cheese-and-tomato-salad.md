<h5>Now on to this week's recipe. This salad is one of the tastier things I've made in a while, and well worth the extra trip to the grocery store for <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halloumi">Halloumi Cheese</a>. For those of who are aren't familiar with this particular lactiferous delicacy, halloumi is a traditional Greek cheese with a high melting point that can be grilled or fried...basically it brings a whole new delicious meaning to "grilled cheese".</h5>
<h5><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/series.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-3464" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/series-1024x225.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="140" /></a></h5>
<h5><em>(Adapted from <a href="http://thedabble.com/blog/halloumi-cheese-tomato-salad-caper-vinaigrette/">The Dabble</a>)</em></h5>
What you need (enough for 2 fairly large servings):
<ul>
	<li>1/2 lb halloumi cheese, cut into 2 slices</li>
	<li>1 lb tomatoes, chopped</li>
	<li>1/3 cup olive oil</li>
	<li>2 cloves of garlic, minced</li>
	<li>2 tsp chopped fresh rosemary</li>
	<li>1/2 cup fresh basil, minced</li>
	<li>1-2 tbs capers</li>
	<li>Kosher salt and freshly ground pepper to taste</li>
</ul>
<div><span style="font-size: small;"><span class="Apple-style-span" style="line-height: 24px;">What you need to do:</span></span></div>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">In a large dish, mix together the oil, garlic, rosemary and capers. Add the slices of cheese, coat them in the marinade, cover and marinate for about 1 hour.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Heat a gast iron skillet on the stove under medium-high heat. Grill the marinated cheese (save the marinade) until it starts to brown (about 3 minutes per side).</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Cut the cheese into bite-sized pieces then mix together with the tomatoes, basil and remaining marinade. Add salt and pepper to taste.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Serve with bread or eat as is. Enjoy!</span></li>
</ol>
</div>
