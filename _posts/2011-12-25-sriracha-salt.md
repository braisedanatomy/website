<h5>I've never met a plate of food I didn't like. I'm not saying that I <em>love</em> all the food I eat, but if you put food in front of me, I'll eat it...and most likely enjoy it. If my taste buds aren't super enthused with the first bite, I typically add one of two things to turn the dish around - salt or sriracha (mmmm sriracha)...Granted, I have a salt tooth (as opposed to sweet tooth) and I tend to think food isn't adequately spicy if my nose isn't actively running, so I might be biased in my opinion of these "cure-alls", but if they're so delicious as individual condiments, why not combine them?</h5>
<h5>Given my obsession with <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sriracha_sauce">Sriracha</a>, I'm a little surprised it took almost a year to get my hands on <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Sriracha-Cookbook-Rooster-Sauce-Recipes/dp/1607740036">The Sriracha Cookbook</a>, but as they say, good things come to those who wait (or those who procrastinate?). This is only my first attempt at one of the 50 rhinorrhea-inducing recipes, and it was well worth the wait. Be sure to stay tuned for more "rooster sauce" flavored treats!</h5>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/salt-steps.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-3524" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/salt-steps-1024x729.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="455" /></a>

<em>(Adapted from <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Sriracha-Cookbook-Rooster-Sauce-Recipes/dp/1607740036">The Sriracha Cookbook</a>)</em>

What you need:
<ul>
	<li>1/2 cup coarse kosher salt</li>
	<li>5 tsp sriracha</li>
</ul>
<div><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 24px;">What you need to do:</span></span></div>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Combine the salt with the sriracha and mix thoroughly.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Spread out the mixture on a baking dish and set in a warm, dry location.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Let dry for 1-2 days (I let it sit out for 2 days, but it was pretty dry after 1 day).</span></li>
</ol>
</div>
