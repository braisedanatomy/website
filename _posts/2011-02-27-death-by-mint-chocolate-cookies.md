What you need (makes about 40 cookies):
<ul>
	<li>4 1/2 cups all purpose flour</li>
	<li>1 cup baking cocoa</li>
	<li>2 1/2 cups sugar</li>
	<li>2 tsp baking powder</li>
	<li>3 cups mini chocolate chips (semi-sweet or bitter-sweet)</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp salt</li>
	<li>2 cups butter (4 sticks) cut into chunks</li>
	<li>4 eggs at room temp</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp mint extract</li>
</ul>
What you need to do:
<ol>
	<li>Whisk together the dry ingredients (flour, cocoa, baking powder and salt) in a bowl and set aside.</li>
	<li>In a different bowl, combine the butter and sugar using a hand mixer. Mix until it reaches a fluffy consistency. This takes about 5 minutes.</li>
	<li>Beat in one egg at a time (mix between each addition).</li>
	<li>Beat in the mint extract.</li>
	<li>Mix in the dry ingredients in 4 batches (beat between each addition).</li>
	<li>Using a spatula, fold in the chocolate chips.</li>
	<li>Refrigerate in a covered bowl for 8 hours (the dough is really sticky so this helps when it comes time to form the cookies).</li>
	<li>Once refrigerated, preheat the oven to 350 degrees, and line the cookie sheet with parchment paper (or use non-stick spray if parchment paper isn't available).</li>
	<li>Divide the dough into meatball sized balls (that's a unit of measurement, right?) and flatten slightly before placing on the cookie sheet.</li>
	<li>Bake for 14 minutes.</li>
	<li>Cool before eating (I'm speaking from experience here).</li>
	<li>Enjoy!</li>
</ol>
