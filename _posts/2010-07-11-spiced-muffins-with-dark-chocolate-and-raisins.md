<div><span style="font-size: 100%;">What you need:</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">3 tbs melted (unsalted) butter</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">2 cups all-purpose flour</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">1/4 cup sugar</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">1/2 tsp salt</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">3 tsp baking powder</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">1 1/2 tsp cinnamon</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">1/2 tsp ground ginger</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">1/2 tsp ground clove</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">1/4 tsp nutmeg</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">1 egg</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">1 cup milk</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">1/2 cup raisins</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">1/2 cup bittersweet (or semisweet) chocolate chips (add more if you want more chocolate)</span></div>
<div><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">What you need to do:</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F and grease a 12-cup muffin tin.</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">2. Combine the flour, sugar, salt, baking powder, cinnamon, ginger, clove and nutmeg in a mixing bowl. Use a whisk to mix the dry ingredients (you want to make sure the spices are evenly distributed).</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">3. Beat the egg in a separate bowl, and add it, along with the milk and melted butter, to the dry ingredients.</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">4. Fold and stir (not beat) the wet and dry ingredients together.</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">5. Once they're thoroughly mixed, add the raisins and chocolate chips.</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">6. Continue to fold the new ingredients into the mixture.</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">6. Spoon the batter into the muffin tin (we recommend using an ice cream scooper).</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">7. Bake for 20 min.</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">
</span><span style="font-size: 100%;">8. Enjoy!</span></div>
