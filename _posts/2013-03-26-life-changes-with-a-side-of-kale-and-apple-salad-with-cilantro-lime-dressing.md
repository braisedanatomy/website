Okay. Now that I've made my excuses for not posting in a while, and finally had to opportunity to tell all of you about the big changes in our lives, I guess I can go ahead and present you with my new favorite recipe - kale and apple salad with cilantro-lime dressing.

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2013/03/Blender.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4489" title="Blender" src="/wp-content/uploads/2013/03/Blender-1024x753.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="470" /></a>

Ingredients:
<ul>
	<li>1 bundle of raw kale, deveined and chopped</li>
	<li>3-4 medium sized apples (<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cripps_Pink">Pink Ladies</a> are great in this salad), chopped</li>
	<li>3 medium sized limes, juiced (about 1/3-1/2 cup fresh lime juice)</li>
	<li>1/3 cup oil (a combination of olive oil and coconut oil is my favorite)</li>
	<li>1 bunch of cilantro, roughly chopped</li>
	<li>1-2 cloves garlic, minced (be careful not too add too much because raw garlic is pretty powerful)</li>
	<li>1 tbs honey</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp salt</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp pepper</li>
</ul>
<ol>
	<li><span style="line-height: 16px;">Add the lime juice, oil, cilantro, garlic, honey, salt and pepper to a blender and blend until the cilantro is pretty much pulverized. </span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 16px;">In a large bowl mix together the chopped kale and apples.</span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 16px;">Pour dressing over salad and toss.</span></li>
</ol>
