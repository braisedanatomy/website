<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/peppersinpan.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-3531" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/peppersinpan-1024x701.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="438" /></a>

What you need:
<ul>
	<li>6 large red or yellow peppers</li>
	<li>12 cloves of garlic, peeled</li>
	<li>Olive oil</li>
	<li>2 mason jars</li>
</ul>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/steps.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-3529" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/steps-754x1024.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="869" /></a>

What you need to do:
<ol>
	<li>Place your 6 peppers in the oven on the middle rack and set it to broil.</li>
	<li>Keep an eye on the peppers and rotate them whenever the top side starts to brown.</li>
	<li>When all four sides are adequately roasted, take them out of the oven and place them in a covered bowl (this is supposed to help loosen the pepper skin).</li>
	<li>After 5-10 minutes, take the peppers out of the bowl and peel off the skin.</li>
	<li>Slice the peppers into long strips and place half of the pepper slices in each of the mason jars.</li>
	<li>Add 6 peeled garlic cloves into each of the mason jars.</li>
	<li>Add enough olive oil to top off each of the jars.</li>
	<li>Screw on the tops and place in the fridge.</li>
	<li>Let the peppers sit in the fridge for at least 3-4 days before you start to eat them.</li>
</ol>
<h5><em>(I’ll admit I don’t know how long these peppers keep in the fridge because my family usually eats them up within 2 weeks. If you want to store them for longer, be sure to can them “properly”.)</em></h5>
