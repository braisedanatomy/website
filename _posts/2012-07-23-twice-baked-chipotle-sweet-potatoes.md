Sometimes I picture this conversation taking place between the sweet potato and the baked potato - "Anything you can do I can do better. I can do anything better than you..."

I don't mean to be rude to the baked potato (trust me, I love a good baked potato), but can you really turn down it's better looking/tasting twin? Okay. Fine. I'll admit it. I'm a potato-ist.

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/series3.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4033" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/series3-1024x696.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="435" /></a>

<span style="color: #ff0000;"><em>(</em></span><span style="color: #ff0000;"><em>Adapted from</em><em></em><em> <a href="http://pinchofyum.com/healthy-mexican-sweet-potato-skins">Pinch of Yum</a>)</em></span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Ingredients (makes 6 potato skins):</span>
<ul>
	<li>3 medium sweet potatoes</li>
	<li>1 can corn, rinsed</li>
	<li>1 can black beans, rinsed</li>
	<li>1/2 yellow onion, diced</li>
	<li>4 oz can of green chilis, diced</li>
	<li>2 tbs cream cheese</li>
	<li>1/4 cup sour cream</li>
	<li>1 tsp salt</li>
	<li>2-3 green onions, diced</li>
	<li>cilantro, chopped</li>
	<li>Shredded cheese</li>
</ul>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Preheat oven to 350 degrees, and bake sweet potatoes for 45-60 minutes (or if you're feeling lazy like I wan, you can microwave each potato for 4 minutes then bake for about 20 minutes).</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Place corn in a heavy cast-iron skillet over medium heat. Sprinkle with salt, cayenne, cumin, coriander, etc (whatever you like). Let corn roast for several minutes before stirring. After stirring, let roast for several more minutes. Continue until roasted to your liking.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Sauté onions in butter until soft.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Once potatoes are cooked, slice in half and scrape out the insides into a separate bowl. Be sure to leave the skins intact. </span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Combine the sweet potato innards (that sounds gross) with the cream cheese, sour cream, green chilis and salt.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Add in the black beans, corn, yellow onions, green onions and cilantro and continue to mix until well combined.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Add the mixture back into the potato skins, top with shredded cheese and cilantro, and bake for another 5 minutes until the cheese is melted.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Serve as an entrée or as a side dish and enjoy!</span></li>
</ol>
</div>
