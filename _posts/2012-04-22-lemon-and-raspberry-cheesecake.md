<h5>Everyone needs their "go to" dessert for potlucks/dinner parties/lazy sunday afternoons, and this is mine.</h5>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/series.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-3847" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/series-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a>

What you need (makes 2 cheesecakes):
<ul>
	<li>2 graham cracker crusts (I'm lazy so I just buy them from the store, but you can also <a href="http://allrecipes.com/recipe/graham-cracker-crust-i/">make them yourself</a>)</li>
	<li>4 packages of cream cheese, warmed to room temperature</li>
	<li>juice from 2 lemons</li>
	<li>1/2 cup of sugar</li>
	<li>1 tbs vanilla</li>
	<li>3 eggs</li>
	<li>4 cups of frozen raspberries</li>
	<li>1 tbs sugar</li>
	<li>1 tbs corn starch (dissolved in water)</li>
</ul>
<div><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 24px;">What you need to do:</span></span></div>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Preheat oven to 325 degrees F.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Combine the cream cheese, lemon juice, sugar, vanilla, and eggs into a large bowl, and mix with a hand mixer until smooth (about 4-5 minutes).</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Divide the filling between the two crusts and bake for 40 minutes.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">While the cakes are in the oven, add the raspberries and sugar to a sauce pan and heat on medium.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Once the raspberries have broken down (about 5 minutes) add the corn starch (don't forget to dissolve it first in water!), stir and continue heating for 1 minute. Set aside to cool until the cakes are done.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Once the cheesecakes are done, add the raspberry topping and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.</span></li>
</ol>
</div>
