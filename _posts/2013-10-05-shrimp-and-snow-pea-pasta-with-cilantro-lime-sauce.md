This recipe needs no introduction. It is a family favorite that's made for any and all get-togethers. It's also one that can guarantee a visit from my sister if she catches wind of dinner plans featuring this delectable entrée.

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/grill.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4697" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/grill-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a>

Ingredients (makes 12-16 servings):
<ul>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">2 lbs Rotini</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">2 lbs. cooked shrimp</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">2 8  oz cans of water chestnuts, chopped</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">1 lb. (or more) sugar snap peas (fresh or thawed from frozen)</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">1 cup chopped green onions</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">2 1/3 cups lime juice – about 10 medium sized limes</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">1 cup olive oil</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">1/2 cup soy sauce</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">1 bunch fresh cilantro, chopped</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">2 Tbs. Mexican seasoning (store bought or <a href="http://allrecipes.com/Recipe/Taco-Seasoning-I/">homemade</a>)</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">4-5 cloves garlic, minced</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Crushed Red Pepper, per taste</span></li>
</ul>
<div><span style="font-size: medium;"><span style="line-height: 21.81818199157715px;"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/chestnuts.jpg"><img class="aligncenter  wp-image-4698" title="putting my sister to work" src="/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/chestnuts-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="512" height="341" /></a></span></span></div>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Whisk together 2 cups of the lime juice, 3/4 cup olive oil, soy sauce, cilantro, green onions and Mexican seasoning till well blended, then set aside.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Sauté the minced garlic with 1/4 tsp red pepper in about 1/4 cup olive oil. Pour most but not all the oil over the shrimp, add 1/3 cup lime juice, toss and set aside.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Trim the  peas into about 3/4 inch pieces. In the same sauté pan, add about 4 Tbs. water and cook the snow peas 1 minute covered, then 2 minutes, stirring. Remove from skillet and set aside.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Grill the shrimp till slightly browned.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Cook the pasta then drain.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Combine the pasta, shrimp, snow peas, and water chestnuts. Add sauce to taste.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 16px;">For the record, this pasta tastes better and better the longer it sits in the sauce. As much as we dig in the moment after everything is done, it's really the left overs we all crave. </span></li>
</ol>
</div>
