What you need (makes about 100 crackers):
<ul>
	<li>1 1/2 cups of grated sharp cheddar</li>
	<li>4 tbs of butter, cut into chunks</li>
	<li>1/2 cup whole wheat flour</li>
	<li>1/4 cup all-purpose flour</li>
	<li>1/4 tsp onion powder</li>
	<li>1 tsp za'atar spice (optional)</li>
	<li>1/4 tsp salt</li>
	<li>fish cookie cutter (you can buy one, or, if you're cheap like me, make one out of folded aluminum foil)</li>
</ul>
What you need to do:
<ol>
	<li>Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F.</li>
	<li>Combine all of the ingredients in a food processor and run the machine until everything is evenly combined into a ball of dough (takes about 2 minutes).</li>
	<li>Roll the dough into a ball and flatten slightly. Cover the dough in plastic warp and place in the freezer for 20-30 min.</li>
	<li>Remove dough from the freezer, and, on a lightly floured surface, roll out to about 1/8 inch thick.</li>
	<li>Use your cookie cutter to form each cracker and place them on an ungreased cookie sheet.</li>
	<li>Bake the crackers for 10-12 minutes (until the edges start the brown).</li>
	<li>Let cool and enjoy!</li>
</ol>
