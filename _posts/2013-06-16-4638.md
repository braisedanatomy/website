<strong>Recipe</strong> (can be found at Smitten Kitchen by <a href="http://smittenkitchen.com/blog/2008/03/almond-biscotti/">clicking here</a>...I know, I know, next week will have a different source!)

I made these as a thank-you gift for all of the help I received for this project.  It is now my go-to for last minute gratitude.  It is about as easy as <a href="/2012/04/01/peanut-butter-banana-bread-in-7-dirty-dishes-video/">banana bread</a>, but more portable -- it doesn't go stale for 1-2 weeks and holds up to shipping (done once already).  Here's my advice:
<ol>
	<li>A 1/2 recipe will suffice for one gift; it makes about 15 biscotti</li>
	<li>During the second bake, don't worry about the biscotti still feeling moist in the oven before you take it out.  If you wait until they're completely hard, they won't be very tender.</li>
	<li>Play with the ingredients...the next time I think a lime &amp; cardamom might be nice.  Anise lends a traditional yet more adventurous taste.</li>
	<li>If you're shipping, ship in an air-tight container, preferably with layers of parchment or wax paper between layers of biscotti.</li>
	<li>This a gift that is especially good for coffee and tea drinkers.</li>
</ol>
