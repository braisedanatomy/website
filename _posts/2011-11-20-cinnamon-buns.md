<h5>Good things come to those who wait...</h5>
<h5>I've waited for 20+ years to reach the point in my education where I could start to understand how the human body works. I'll wait patiently through a 8 hour surgery for the 10 minutes during which I get the opportunity to show off my mad suturing skills (mind you, those sutures should really take me 2 minutes...). So why not wait 2 hours for some delicious fresh-from-the-oven homemade cinnamon buns? The 500+ calories of sugary goodness make it totally worth it.</h5>
[gallery orderby="title"]

<em>(Adapted from <a href="http://allrecipes.com/Recipe/clone-of-a-cinnabon/detail.aspx">Clone of a Cinnabon</a>)</em>

What you need:
<ul>
	<li>1 cup warm milk (110 degrees F)</li>
	<li>2 eggs at room temperature</li>
	<li>1/3 cup butter, melted</li>
	<li>4 1/2 cups bread flour (bread flour makes the bus fluffier)</li>
	<li>1 tsp salt</li>
	<li>1/2 cup white sugar</li>
	<li>2 1/2 teaspoons yeast</li>
	<li>1 cup brown sugar, packed</li>
	<li>2 1/2 tbs ground cinnamon</li>
	<li>1/3 cup butter, softened</li>
	<li>4 ounces cream cheese</li>
	<li>1 tbs milk</li>
	<li>1 tsp vanilla extract</li>
	<li>1 cup confectioner's sugar</li>
	<li>1/8 tsp salt</li>
</ul>
<div><span style="font-size: small;"><span class="Apple-style-span" style="line-height: 24px;">What you need to do:</span></span></div>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Place the warm milk, eggs, 1/3 cup melted butter, bread flour, 1 tsp salt, white sugar, and yeast in a bread machine in the order recommended by the manufacturer (I just added everything in the order listed above). Set the machine to "dough cycle" and press start.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">After the dough has doubled in size, turn it out onto a lightly floured surface, cover and let sit for 10 minutes.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">In a separate bowl, combine the brown sugar and cinnamon.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Roll the dough out into a 16 by 21 inch rectangle.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Take the 1/3 cup softened butter and spread it evenly over the rolled out dough.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Sprinkle the cinnamon sugar over the butter covered dough.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Roll the dough into a long tube, and cut it into 12 pieces.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Place the rolls in a lightly greased 9 by 13 inch baking pan, cover and let rise for another 30 minutes.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Bake the rolls in the oven until golden brown (about 15 minutes).</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">While the rolls are in the oven, combine the cream cheese, milk, vanilla extract, confectioner's sugar, and 1/8 tsp salt.</span></li>
	<li><span class="Apple-style-span" style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">When the rolls are done baking, coat them with frosting and get ready to enter a food coma.</span></li>
</ol>
</div>
