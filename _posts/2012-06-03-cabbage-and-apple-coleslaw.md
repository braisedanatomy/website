<h5>As the weather gets warmer, I find myself switching from my usual cravings of warm, comfort food to something a little more refreshing...maybe even with a little bit of crunch. Coleslaw definitely falls into the category of a crunchy summer favorite, but I always find it a little bit lacking when it comes to feeling refreshed. The cabbage is right one target, but when the mayo hits my tastebuds, I get bogged down with the creaminess and am left craving a large glass of [insert age appropriate beverage]. Anita has already presented you with <a href="/2011/02/23/sesame-coleslaw/">one tasty alternative</a> to traditional coleslaw, and here is my attempt and accomplishing this task.</h5>
Ingredients:
<ul>
	<li>1/2 head of green cabbage</li>
	<li>1/4 head of purple cabbage</li>
	<li>4-6 peeled carrots, thinly sliced</li>
	<li>2 apples, cut into matchsticks</li>
	<li>1/2 cup rice vinegar</li>
	<li>1/4 cup sesame oil</li>
	<li>2 tbs brown sugar</li>
	<li>toasted sesame seeds, <em>optional</em></li>
</ul>
<span style="line-height: 24px;">Instructions:</span>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Thinly slice the cabbage, <a href="/2011/02/23/sesame-coleslaw/">per Anita's instructions</a>.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Add the cabbage, carrots and apples to a large bowl.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">In a separate bowl, whisk together the vinegar, sesame oil and brown sugar, then pour over the coleslaw.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Sprinkle toasted sesame on top and enjoy!</span></li>
</ol>
