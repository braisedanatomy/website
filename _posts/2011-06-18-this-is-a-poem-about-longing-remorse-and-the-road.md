<em>This stretch of I-70 is 400 miles
9 hours from St. Louis to Cleveland and
I’m about 5 hours in</em>

<em>Utter fullness never felt so defeating
and I’m beginning to regret that fleeting
craving for that McMuffin
and for you, dear Cup o’ Joe</em>

<em>Exit 63 was the devil on the shoulder of my road</em>

<em>Every bump on the highway
with you inside me, changing all the while</em>

<em>I don’t have time to stop again
but my friend
your persistence in escape blurs the lines on the road</em>

<em>I look at the empty cup and wish I were a guy,
that its golden arches might mingle with mine </em>
