<h5>Smoked salmon (aka lox), basil and avocado - 'nuff said. The only thing I'll add next time I make this sandwich is a generous helping of capers.</h5>
<h5>Side note - some of you may have noticed that Anita and I often reference <a href="http://www.freakyfast.com/2011/11/remember-the-day-old-bread-it-rox.html">Jimmy John's "day old bread"</a> (example <a href="/2012/03/11/spinach-feta-strata/">#1</a>, <a href="/2010/08/29/tomato-onion-and-basil-bruschetta/">#2</a>, <a href="/2010/11/21/tomato-and-garlic-salsabruschetta-aka-salschetta/">#3</a>, <a href="/2010/09/05/pumpkin-soup-and-sage-toast/">#4</a>). It an absolute steal at 50 cents per loaf and is perfect for sandwiches, spreads, dips, etc.</h5>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/bread.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-3910" title="one of the tastiest thing you can buy for 50 cents." src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/bread-300x200.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="200" /></a>What you need:
<ul>
	<li>Bread (jimmy john's day old bread if it's available!)</li>
	<li>Smoked Salmon</li>
	<li>Avocado</li>
	<li>Fresh basil</li>
	<li>Olive oil</li>
	<li>Sea salt, to taste</li>
	<li>Freshly ground pepper, to taste</li>
</ul>
<div><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 24px;">What you need to do:</span></span></div>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Slice and toast your bread.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Layer the bread with thinly sliced avocado, smoked salmon, and fresh basil.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Drizzle with olive oil, and sprinkle with sea salt and cracked pepper.</span></li>
</ol>
</div>
