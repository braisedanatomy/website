Nothing quite says "Thank You" like a batch of homemade brownies...

Here some are simple-to-follow instructions that will show you how to a homemade brownie mix. It only takes about 15 minutes, and it's a great gift for anyone who likes chocolate...so basically, all of mankind.

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/ingredients.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-2183" title="those are the same notecards I use to make my study flashcards..." src="/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/ingredients-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a>

What you need:
<ul>
	<li>1 cup flour</li>
	<li>1/2 cup baking cocoa (regular or dark)</li>
	<li>1 cup granulated sugar</li>
	<li>1/2 - 1 cup chocolate chips (add at your discretion)</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp salt</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp baking powder</li>
	<li>1 canning jar</li>
	<li>notecards (and <a href="http://www.amazon.com/STD334SB10-Fineliner-Porous-Point-Assorted/dp/B000RMS238/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&amp;qid=1300041398&amp;sr=8-1">colorful pens</a>)</li>
</ul>
What you need to do:
<ol>
	<li>Add the ingredients to canning jar in the order listed above. I recommend using a funnel to minimize spillage (I made a funnel out of a sheet of paper and it worked just fine).</li>
	<li>Attach to the jar a card that reads:</li>
</ol>
Additional Ingredients:
<ul>
	<li>1/2 cup (1 stick) butter</li>
	<li>2 eggs</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp vanilla</li>
</ul>
Instructions:
<ol>
	<li>Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F</li>
	<li>Place the butter in a small mixing bowl and microwave until melted</li>
	<li>Combine the butter with the eggs and vanilla</li>
	<li>In a separate bowl, mix together the contents of this jar</li>
	<li>Combine the dry ingredients with the eggs, vanilla and butter and mix until smooth</li>
	<li>Pour in a baking pan</li>
	<li>Bake for about 25 minutes (be sure to test with a toothpick)</li>
</ol>
