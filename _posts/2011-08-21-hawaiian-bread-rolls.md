<em>(Adapted from <a href="http://www.handletheheat.com/2011/07/homemade-kitchen-hawaiian-bread-rolls.html">Handle the Heat</a>)</em>

What you need:
<ul>
	<li>1/2 cup warm water</li>
	<li>1/2 cup pineapple juice</li>
	<li>1/4 cup melted butter (plus a little extra to brush the rolls)</li>
	<li>1 tbs dry yeast</li>
	<li>1 large egg, beaten</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp vanilla extract</li>
	<li>6 tbs sugar</li>
	<li>3 cups all-purpose flour</li>
</ul>
What you need to do:
<ol>
	<li>In a large bowl, combine the water, pineapple juice and melted butter.</li>
	<li>Sprinkle in the yeast.</li>
	<li>Add the sugar, salt, egg and vanilla then stir to combine all of the ingredients.</li>
	<li>Mix in 1 cup of flour at a time with a spoon.</li>
	<li>Cover the dough with a towel and let rise in a warm spot until it doubles in size (takes about 2 hours).</li>
	<li>Once the dough has risen, spray two cake pans (about 9-inches in size) with non-strick spray.</li>
	<li>Shape the dough into 16 balls and place 8 of them in each cake pan.</li>
	<li>Cover the pans with a towel and let rise till they double their size (again, about 2 hours).</li>
	<li>Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F.</li>
	<li>Brush the rolls with melted butter and then bake them for 20-25 minutes (until golden brown).</li>
	<li>Let cool and serve with <a href="/2011/07/31/quinoa-burgers/">quinoa burgers</a>.</li>
</ol>
