<h5><span style="color: #444444;"><span style="color: #000000;">3. Italian or </span><a href="http://www.google.com/imgres?imgurl=http://www.seriouseats.com/images/20090723eggplants.png&amp;imgrefurl=http://www.seriouseats.com/2009/07/seriously-italian-eggplant-a-fungetielli-penne-pasta-recipe.html&amp;usg=__M5E6a6QCwqVKUoGdIdbQtqCRJp4=&amp;h=341&amp;w=500&amp;sz=132&amp;hl=en&amp;start=0&amp;tbnid=Sjp9B6wjbxn1BM:&amp;tbnh=129&amp;tbnw=178&amp;prev=/images%3Fq%3Ditalian%2Beggplant%26um%3D1%26hl%3Den%26safe%3Doff%26sa%3DN%26biw%3D1440%26bih%3D740%26tbs%3Disch:1&amp;um=1&amp;itbs=1&amp;iact=hc&amp;vpx=1127&amp;vpy=115&amp;dur=556&amp;hovh=185&amp;hovw=272&amp;tx=156&amp;ty=101&amp;ei=XOtqTN2ULYqRnAeS7s30AQ&amp;oei=XOtqTN2ULYqRnAeS7s30AQ&amp;esq=1&amp;page=1&amp;ndsp=30&amp;ved=1t:429,r:6,s:0"><span style="color: #000000;">Globe Eggplant</span></a></span><span style="color: #444444;"><span style="color: #000000;">.  What appeared in our </span><a href="http://cityfresh.org/"><span style="color: #000000;">City Fresh</span></a><span style="color: #000000;"> pickup this week, last week, and the week before that.  'Tis the season, apparently.</span></span></h5>
<h5><span style="color: #000000;">We're waiting for particularly good recipes for Eggplant Parmesan and Baba Ganoush to fall into our laps.  Until then, we've been charged with making something edible. </span><span style="color: #000000;">Eggplant may play a filler role in this and many other dishes, but after a </span><a href="http://www.whfoods.com/genpage.php?tname=foodspice&amp;dbid=22"><span style="color: #000000;">background check</span></a><span style="color: #000000;"> you'll find that it's not so bad to have around. </span><span style="color: #000000;">Try this...salsa...that uses eggplant rather than tomatoes.</span></h5>
<h5><span style="color: #000000;">Ingredients </span></h5>
<ul>
	<li><span style="font-size: 13px; color: #000000; line-height: 19px;"><span style="color: #000000;">1 medium globe eggplant</span></span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 13px; color: #000000; line-height: 19px;"><span style="color: #000000;"> </span>1 red bell pepper </span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 13px; color: #000000; line-height: 19px;">1/2 small onion, chopped</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 13px; color: #000000; line-height: 19px;">1-2 cloves garlic, minced fine</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 13px; color: #000000; line-height: 19px;">1 Tbsp olive oil + 1 Tbsp red wine vinegar + 1 Tbsp mayonnaise   mixed well</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 13px; color: #000000; line-height: 19px;">2 tsp cumin </span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 13px; color: #000000; line-height: 19px;">2-3 tsp red chili flakes </span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 13px; color: #000000; line-height: 19px;">2 Tbsp chopped parsley </span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 13px; color: #000000; line-height: 19px;">liberal kosher salt and pepper</span></li>
</ul>
<h5>Preheat your oven to 500F.  Place the eggplant and the red pepper on a baking pan, and roast in the oven for 30-45 minutes (if you've got a grill, send the veggies straight to Hades).  Turn 2-3 times with tongs.  When ready, the flesh of each should be soft and the outsides charred.  You can let both cool on their own before skinning them, or use the paper bag method (putting them in closed paper lunch sacks for 10 minutes will steam the skin off).  Chop both fairly well, and then mix in the other ingredients with a spoon until they're distributed.</h5>
