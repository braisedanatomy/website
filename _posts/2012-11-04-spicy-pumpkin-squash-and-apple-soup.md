Ingredients (makes about 5-6 servings)
<ul>
	<li>2 butternut squash (peeled, seeded and cut into small cubes)</li>
	<li>2 tart apples (peeled, cored and cubed)</li>
	<li>2 cups <a href="/2012/10/16/sweet-and-sriracha-pumpkin-seeds-and-anatomy-of-a-pumpkin/">pumpkin purée</a></li>
	<li>6 cups vegetable/chicken broth</li>
	<li>1 onion, roughly chopped</li>
	<li>4-5 garlic cloves, minced</li>
	<li>1-2 tbs olive oil</li>
	<li>1 tbs sriracha</li>
	<li>2 tbs honey</li>
	<li>2 tsp cumin</li>
	<li>1 tsp chili powder</li>
	<li>1 tsp thyme</li>
	<li>1 tsp crushed red pepper</li>
	<li>1 tsp salt</li>
	<li>1 cup half and half</li>
</ul>
<ol>
	<li>In a large soup pot, sauté the onions in olive oil.</li>
	<li>Once the onions begin to soften, add the garlic and continue sautéing until the garlic becomes fragrant.</li>
	<li>Add the squash, apples, pumpkin, broth, sriracha, honey, cumin, chili powder, crushed red pepper, and salt to the pot.</li>
	<li>Bring to a boil then simmer on low for 30 minutes.</li>
	<li>Once the squash is soft, remove the soup from heat and and purée in a blender 2-3 cups at a time, or with a immersion blender.</li>
	<li>Return the puréed soup to the pot, add the half and half and continue cooking for 3-4 minutes.</li>
	<li>Let cool, serve and enjoy!</li>
</ol>
