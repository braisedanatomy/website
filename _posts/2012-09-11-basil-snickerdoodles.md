<h5>In keeping with the recent basil theme, I'm excited to present you with a basil-y twist on an old favorite - <a href="/2010/12/12/snickerdoodles/">the snickerdoodle</a>. But do you really want to mess with butter-y, sugar-y, cinnamon-y perfection? Yes...if you get to add basil-y to the description.</h5>
<h5><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/basil1.jpg"><img title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/basil1-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a></h5>
<h5>And this isn't the last basil inspired recipe you should expect to see in the next couple weeks. As summer draws to a close (I hate to admit it, but it's true), I no longer feel guilty cutting down my basil plant in preparation for transferring to my mom's awesome thriftstore find of an aerogarden for $3. Win (on both accounts)!</h5>
<h5><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/series.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4113" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/series-1024x685.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="428" /></a><em><span style="color: #ff0000;">(Adapted from <a href="http://www.dessertsforbreakfast.com/2012/07/peach-frozen-yogurt-and-basil.html"><span style="color: #ff0000;">Desserts for Breakfast</span></a>)</span></em></h5>
Ingredients:
<ul>
	<li>3 cups all purpose flour</li>
	<li>1 tsp baking soda</li>
	<li>1 tsp cream of tartar</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp salt</li>
	<li>1 cup (2 sticks) butter, softened</li>
	<li>1 1/2 cup sugar</li>
	<li>1/2 cup loosely packed basil, finely chopped</li>
	<li>2 eggs</li>
	<li>1 tsp vanilla</li>
	<li>Cinnamon Sugar (4 tbs sugar, 1 tbs cinnamon)</li>
</ul>
<ol>
	<li>Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F, and grease or line your cookie sheet with a silicon mat/parchment paper.</li>
	<li>In a large bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, cream of tartar and salt.</li>
	<li>In a separate bowl, beat butter for 30 seconds, then add sugar and beat until fluffy (2-3 minutes).</li>
	<li>Add the eggs and vanilla to the butter and sugar mixture; beat well.</li>
	<li>Add the butter mixture and basil to the dry ingredients, and mix for another minute or so.</li>
	<li>Form dough into tablespoon-sized balls and roll in the cinnamon sugar mixture.</li>
	<li>Place 2 inches apart on sheet.</li>
	<li>Bake for about 8-10 minutes, let cool and enjoy!</li>
</ol>
