<h5>My friend Liza has this foolproof recipe for banana bread.  I asked her for, and misplaced, the recipe four or five times before I finally just included it in my family's recipe collection.  Whenever I see overripe bananas I think of that bread.  Whenever I think about that bread, I remember sitting in Liza's room during undergrad, eating most of the loaf and talking about <span style="text-decoration: line-through;">guys</span> philosophy.</h5>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/PBbanana1.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-319" title="The A Team" src="/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/PBbanana1-1024x682.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="426" /></a>
<h5>The original recipe is pretty darn seductive.  I wanted to make it again the other day, but I only had two bananas.  So I added some peanut butter.  And the taste?</h5>
<h5>Awwwwwwww yeah.  <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mhN93rFZuJs">It's business time</a>.</h5>
<address>Ingredients</address>
<ul>
	<li> <address>1 1/2 cups flour</address></li>
	<li> <address>1 tsp baking soda</address></li>
	<li> <address>1/2 tsp salt</address></li>
	<li> <address>1 tsp cinnamon</address></li>
	<li> <address>1/3 cup melted butter</address></li>
	<li> <address>1 egg, slightly beaten</address></li>
	<li> <address>2 bananas, mushed into a paste</address></li>
	<li> <address>1/3 cup peanut butter</address></li>
	<li> <address>1 cup sugar</address></li>
	<li> <address>1 tsp vanilla extract</address></li>
	<li> <address>1 small handful crushed walnuts (optional)</address></li>
</ul>
<h5>Preheat the oven to 350F.  Combine the flour, salt, and baking soda well in a small bowl.  Combine all of the other ingredients in a large bowl.  Add the dry ingredients to the wet (carefully!) and mix until just combined--don't overstir or the batter will become stiff.  Mix in the walnuts now if you're using them.  Grease a loaf pan (or get sassy with some Pam and parchment paper) and bake on the middle rack for 1 hour or until a knife in the middle comes out without wet batter.  Transfer as soon as possible from the loaf pan to a cooling rack.  It tastes best the next day.</h5>
