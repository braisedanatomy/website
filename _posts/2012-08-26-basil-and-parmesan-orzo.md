Not much to say about this recipe except to point out the key ingredients - basil, parmesan and orzo pasta. Yum. There's next to no prep time involved, it's quick to cook, and can be served as a side dish, or if you love basil and parmesan as much as I do, an entrée.

(Adapted from <a href="http://mytinyoven.blogspot.com/2011/06/orzo-with-parmesan-and-basil.html">My Tiny Oven</a>)

Ingredients:
<ul>
	<li>1 1/2 cup uncooked orzo</li>
	<li>3 tbs butter</li>
	<li>2 cloves garlic, minced</li>
	<li>3 cups stock (I love <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Better-Bouillon-Vegan-Chicken-ounce/dp/B001HTJFGI">Better than Bullion</a>)</li>
	<li>1/2 cup basil, chiffoned</li>
	<li>1 cup parmesan cheese</li>
	<li>Salt and pepper to taste</li>
</ul>
<div>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">In a pot, melt the butter and add the orzo to sauté until the orzo is golden brown.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Add the garlic and sauté for an extra minute or two. </span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Add the broth, cover and simmer for 20 minutes.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Remove from the heat once all the broth is absorbed.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: 12px; line-height: 18px;">Add the basil, parmesan, salt and pepper and stir until the cheese is melted.</span></li>
</ol>
</div>
