<h5><em>Quick side note, for those of you who aren't big fans of collard greens, you could also use kale, spinach, or some other dark leafy vegetable.</em></h5>
<h5>(Adapted from <a href="http://veganyumyum.com/2008/06/sweet-chili-lime-tofu-with-wok-steamed-collards-and-quinoa/">Vegan Yum Yum</a>)</h5>
<span style="font-size: small;">What you need for the tofu:</span>
<ul>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">1 block extra firm tofu (about 14 oz)</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 19px;">3 tbs sugar</span></span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 19px;">3 tbs tamari (or soy)</span></span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 19px;">2 tbs fresh lime juice</span></span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 19px;">zest from 1/2 of a lime</span></span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 19px;">1 clove of garlic, minced</span></span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 19px;">1/2 tsp salt</span></span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 19px;">4 mint leaves, finely chopped</span></span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, 'Bitstream Charter', serif; line-height: 15px;"><span style="font-size: small;">For the quinoa:</span></span>
<div>
<ul>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">1 cup uncooked quinoa</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">Zest from 1/2 of a lime</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">2 bruised cardamom pods</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">1/4 tsp salt</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">1 2/3 cup water</span></li>
</ul>
</div>
<span style="font-size: small;"><span style="font-size: small;">For the collar greens:</span></span>
<ul>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">1 bunch collard greens, middle veins removed </span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 19px;">3 tbs water</span></span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 19px;">1 pinch salt</span></span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;"><span style="line-height: 19px;">1 tbs lime juice</span></span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-size: small;">
What you need to do:</span>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">In a pot, mix together all of the ingredients for the quinoa and cover with a tight fitting lid.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">Set the pot on the stove, bring the water to a boil and then turn down the heat to simmer for 20 minutes (or until the quinoa is tender).</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">While the quinoa is cooking, mix together the ingredients for the tofu glaze (everything in the first list minus the tofu). Whisk together the ingredients until the sugar and salt are dissolved.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">Slice the tofu into thin triangles and "<a href="http://chinesefood.about.com/od/tofurecipes/ht/How-To-Dry-Fry-Tofu.htm">dry fry</a>" them by adding the pieces in a single layer to a non-greased pan and pressing down on the tofu so that it cooks in it's own juices (that sounds wrong...). I recommend using a cast iron skillet if you have one handy, but a non-stick pan should work too. Once the tofu has browned on one side, flip the pieces over and do so the same on the other side.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">Once the tofu is "fried", add the sauce and stir to coat the tofu. Turn off the heat, and let the sauce reduce a little (it will get stickier and form a glaze).</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">For the collard greens, remove the "middle vein", stack the leaves, and then cut them into thin slices.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">Add the greens to a wok with the water, lime juice and salt. Cover with a lid and cook on high for about 4 minutes until tender).</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-size: small;">Plate the quinoa, tofu and collard greens and enjoy!</span></li>
</ol>
