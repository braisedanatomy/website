<h5>With my week long trip to seattle more than half over, I have one very concrete opinion formed - it rains a lot (now is that an opinion or a fact??). What does rain make me crave? Warm, comforting soup. And with that, I am happy to present to you one of the tastiest, warm-you-from-the-inside-out soups.</h5>
What you need:
<ul>
	<li>3 tbs butter</li>
	<li>5 cloves garlic, minced</li>
	<li>1 medium onion, minced</li>
	<li>3 tbs flour</li>
	<li>2 28 oz crushed tomato</li>
	<li>3 cups broth (chicken or otherwise)</li>
	<li>2 tbs honey</li>
	<li>2 tsp salt</li>
	<li>handful of basil, chiffoned (or 1.5 tbs dried basil)</li>
	<li>1 cup half and half</li>
	<li>black pepper to taste</li>
</ul>
What you need to do:
<ol>
	<li>Melt the butter in a large pot and sauté the garlic and onions until the onions start the soften.</li>
	<li>Add the flour to the pot and stir. It’s going to thicken up, but don’t worry. That’s what supposed to happen with a roux.</li>
	<li>Add the crushed tomatoes, broth, honey, salt and basil to the pot. Stir to combine.</li>
	<li>Bring the soup to a boil and turn down the heat to simmer for 5-10 minutes.</li>
	<li>Add the half and half when the soup has 2 minutes left to go.</li>
	<li>Serve with toast or grilled cheese and enjoy!</li>
</ol>
