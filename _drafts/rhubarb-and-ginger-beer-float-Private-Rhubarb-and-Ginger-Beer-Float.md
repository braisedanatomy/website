Ingredients (sorbet adapted from <a href="http://simplyrecipes.com/recipes/rhubarb_sorbet/">Simply Recipes</a>)
<ul>
	<li>3 1/2 cups of chopped fresh rhubarb (4-5 stalks)</li>
	<li>2 1/2 cups of water</li>
	<li>1 2/3 cups of sugar</li>
	<li>1/4 teaspoon of salt</li>
	<li>1 Tbsp orange zest</li>
	<li>1 Tbsp of chopped fresh ginger</li>
	<li>2 tablespoons of corn syrup (I used light)</li>
	<li>a few bottles of ginger beer.  I like Regatta brand</li>
</ul>
<h5>This makes about a quart.  If you don't have an ice cream maker, you can put the mixture into ice cube trays or popsicle mold.  Put all of the ingredients <strong>besides the corn syrup</strong> into a medium-sized pot and bring to a boil.  Cover and simmer for 5 minutes, then let cool to room temperature.  Stir in the corn syrup, and store in the fridge for a few hours or overnight until thoroughly chilled.  Follow your ice cream maker's directions to make the sorbet.  When it it first made, it will be soft and melty.  Store in an airtight container in the freezer for a few hours to make it more solid and scoopable.  Fill a glass with ginger beer, and scoop in a few scoops of the sorbet.  Serve immediately.</h5>
