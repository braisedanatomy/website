<div>Adapted from <a href="http://www.justapinch.com/recipes/greek-yogurt-strawberry-banana-bread-healthy-and-delicious.html?p=5">Just A Pinch</a></div>
<div>Ingredients</div>
<div>
<div>1.5 cups whole wheat flour</div>
<div>1.5 tsp baking powder</div>
<div>1/2 tsp kosher salt</div>
<div>1/2 cup brown sugar</div>
<div>3 ripe bananas</div>
<div>2 eggs</div>
<div>6 oz. strawberry greek yogurt</div>
<div>1 lb diced fresh strawberries</div>
</div>
<div>
<div></div>
<div>Instructions</div>
<div><span style="font-size: 12px;">Preheat oven to 375 degrees. Start by combining flour, baking powder, and salt in a large mixing bowl.</span></div>
<div>
<div>In a separate bowl, mash bananas with a fork until they are gooey and liquid-like.</div>
<div>Add eggs and sugar and mix thoroughly.</div>
<div>Add greek yogurt</div>
<div>Whisk until everything is well combined and there are no lumps.</div>
<div>Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients, and mix until just combined. At this point, fold in the fresh strawberries!</div>
<div>Pour batter into a greased loaf pan and distribute evenly.</div>
<div>Put into the oven and bake for one hour, or until the top is golden brown and a toothpick comes out clean (55 minutes for me).</div>
<div>Transfer to a cooling rack and (try to) let the bread cool for at least ten minutes before slicing.</div>
</div>
</div>
