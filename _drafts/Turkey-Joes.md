Sloppy Joes:
<div>Combine equal parts of the following seasonings - onion powder, garlic powder, coriander, salt, pepper and crushed red pepper flakes, and set aside for later (I made a lot and saved it for next time).</div>
<div>Sauté one diced large onion and one diced bell pepper in olive oil. Add one tablespoon of the mixed seasoning, 2 tablespoons of brown sugar, 1 tablespoon of worcestershire sauce and an 8 oz can of tomato sauce (instead of making sauce ahead of time, I'd just do crushed tomatoes, oregano, and a little extra salt and sugar). Mix then add one pound of meat (I used ground turkey). Simmer for about 5-7 minutes (or longer if you want it less saucy).</div>
<div>I served it on onion buns with swiss cheese or blue cheese. Yummmmmm.</div>
