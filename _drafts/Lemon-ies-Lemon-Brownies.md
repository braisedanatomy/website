<strong>The "Brownie" Batter</strong>
<ul>
	<li>3/4 cup all-purpose flour {King Arthur All-Purpose Flour}</li>
	<li>3/4 cup granulated sugar</li>
	<li>1/4 teaspoon salt {Sea Salt}</li>
	<li>1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, softened</li>
	<li>2 large eggs</li>
	<li>2 tablespoons lemon zest</li>
	<li>2 tablespoons lemon juice</li>
</ul>
<div>
<div>
<div></div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div><strong>Tart Lemon Glaze</strong></div>
</div>
</div>
<ul>
	<li>1 rounded cup powdered sugar</li>
	<li>4 tablespoon lemon juice</li>
	<li>8 teaspoons lemon zest</li>
</ul>
<div>
<div>
<div>Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease/Spray an 8-by-8-inch baking dish with butter/cooking spray and set aside.</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div></div>
<div>Zest and juice two small/large lemons; set aside. {whatever you have}</div>
<div></div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>In the bowl of an electric mixture fitted with the paddle attachment, beat the flour, sugar, salt, and softened butter until combined.</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div></div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>In a separate bowl, whisk together the eggs, lemon zest, and lemon juice until combined. Pour into the flour mixture and beat at medium speed until smooth and creamy, about 2 minutes.</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div></div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div>Pour into baking dish and bake for 23-25 minutes, or until just starting to turn golden around the edges and a toothpick inserted into the center of the brownies comes out clean. Allow to cool completely before glazing.  Do not overbake, or the bars will be dry. {even with the yummy glaze}</div>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<div>
<div></div>
</div>
</div>
<div>When brownies are cooled completely, make the glaze...sift the powdered sugar, add lemon zest and juice, and whisk together all three ingredients. Spread 1/2 the glaze over the brownies with a rubber spatula.  Let glaze set.  Spread the remaining glaze over the bars, and let it set.  This glaze does not harden like most.  Cut into bars, and serve!</div>
