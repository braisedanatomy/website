Ingredients
<ul>
	<li>1/2 cup crunchy peanut butter</li>
	<li>juice of 1 lime</li>
	<li>1 Tbsp soy sauce</li>
	<li>1/2 tsp chili powder</li>
	<li>salt to taste</li>
</ul>
<h5>Put everything into a tupperware and shake to mix.  Add water to loosen the sauce so that you can pour it over chicken or tofu.</h5>
