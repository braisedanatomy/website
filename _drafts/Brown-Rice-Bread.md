Ingredients
<ul>
	<li>cooked brown rice</li>
	<li>whole wheat flour</li>
	<li>packet yeast</li>
	<li>vital wheat gluten</li>
	<li>salt</li>
	<li>water</li>
</ul>
<h5>In a large bowl, use a spoon to mix the dry ingredients together.  Add the water and mix into a wet, formless batter.  Cover with a wet towel and let rise in a warm environment (warmer than 70 degrees) for 2 hours.  Afterward, cover with a lid or plastic wrap and store in the fridge between 24 hours and 7 days.  The day of baking, dust the counter with flour.  Take out half of the dough, form into a loose ball, and let rise on the floured counter under plastic wrap for 90 minutes.  60 minutes into the rise, set the oven for 450F.  Put a cast iron skillet, pizza stone, or baking tray on the middle rack and a metal baking dish on the bottom rack.  After the 90 minute rise, transfer the dough to the skillet/stone/tray and put 1 cup of hot water into the baking dish.  Bake for 30 minutes, less if your oven is more powerful.  Test the bread by knocking on it like a door.  Hollow = done.  The bread tastes best after it has cooled completely, sliced and slightly toasted with some melted butter and/or jam.</h5>
