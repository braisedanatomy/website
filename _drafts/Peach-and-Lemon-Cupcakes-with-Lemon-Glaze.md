<span style="text-decoration: underline;">For the donuts:</span>
1 1/2 cups flour
1/2 cup sugar
1 teaspoon baking powder
1/2 teaspoon baking soda
1/4 teaspoon salt
1 egg
6 oz peach yogurt (I used one container of Chobani lemon and it was perfect)
1/3 cup of almond milk (or soy...or regular...)
1/4 cup canola oil
zest of 1 lemon

Juice from 1/2 lemon
<span style="text-decoration: underline;">For the glaze</span>:
1 cup powdered sugar
juice of 1 lemon
